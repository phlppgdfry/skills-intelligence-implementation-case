# Employee FAQ

*Plain language, for the intranet and the Teams app's "About" tab. Translated into NL, FR and DE before launch; the Dutch and German openings are below.*

**What is this?**
A tool that helps you and HR see your skills, so you hear about roles and projects that fit you, also in the other company of the group.

**Where do the suggestions come from?**
From four places: your job profile, Jira tickets you finished (only the title, the date and the component), courses you completed, and the "about me" text you wrote yourself in SuccessFactors. Every suggestion shows the exact sentence it came from.

**Does it read my Teams chats, emails or Slack?**
No. Teams is only used to send you the question and to receive your answer.

**What happens when I click "No"?**
The skill is removed from your suggestions and is not asked again for 12 months. If we had added it to your Workday profile before, we remove it.

**What if I don't answer?**
Nothing is written anywhere. You get one reminder after 7 days. After 21 days the card expires.

**Who sees my skills?**
You see everything. Your manager and HR see only skills you confirmed. Reports for the workforce plan only show groups of 10 people or more.

**Can this be used in my evaluation?**
No. The agreement with the works council excludes performance evaluation, discipline and dismissal decisions.

**Can I say no to all of it?**
Yes. Use the opt-out in the app or ask HR. From the next night on, none of your data is processed, and inferred data is deleted within 30 days.

**I was at Keldorf. Why are my skills not in Workday?**
If you still work for a Keldorf entity, your HR profile is in SuccessFactors. Your confirmed skills are kept and used for mobility, and they move to Workday with the planned migration.
